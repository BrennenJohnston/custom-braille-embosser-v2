# Design files

Version 2 of the Custom Braille Embosser is published as STL files only. The nine printed parts are in [`Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/STL`](../Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/STL), and every part in its assembled position is in [`Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/Assembly_Reference`](../Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/Assembly_Reference).

I have not published CAD source (no Fusion archive, no STEP) for Version 2: the parts were exported as STL only. Version 1's Fusion 360 archive and STEP files are in the [Version 1 repository](https://github.com/BrennenJohnston/custom-braille-embosser-3d-printing-project), and the Version 2 parts grew out of that design.

The two braille cylinders are not design files here at all: they are generated for each message by the [web app](https://braille-cylinder-stl-generator.vercel.app), whose source is the [braille-cylinder-stl-generator](https://github.com/BrennenJohnston/braille-cylinder-stl-generator) repository, with an OpenSCAD alternative in [braille-cylinder-stl-generator-openscad](https://github.com/BrennenJohnston/braille-cylinder-stl-generator-openscad).
