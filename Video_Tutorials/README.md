# Video tutorials

I recorded the Version 2 assembly on 2026-10-01, but the recording is raw, with repeated takes, and I am not publishing it until it is edited and voiced. This page will carry the finished video's link, its captions file and a written version of what it shows.

Until then, the written guides stand on their own: the [Maker Guide](../Documentation/Step_2_Guide_Option_3_Custom_Braille_Embosser_V2_Maker_Guide.md) has every step in a few pages, the [Assembly Guide By Touch](../Documentation/Step_2_Guide_Option_2_Custom_Braille_Embosser_V2_Assembly_Guide_By_Touch.md) says every step for the hands, and the [Visual Assembly Guide](../Documentation/Step_2_Guide_Option_1_Custom_Braille_Embosser_V2_Visual_Assembly_Guide.md) shows it without words.

## The rendered assembly animation

`Custom_Braille_Embosser_V2_Assembly_Animation.mp4` is the whole build in 48 seconds, and `steps/` holds one clip a step. They are rendered from the part files by `scripts/animate_assembly.py`, not filmed. `Custom_Braille_Embosser_V2_Assembly_Animation_Short.mp4` is the same build in 28 seconds, for MakerWorld, which takes a video under 30 seconds, and `..._Short.gif` the same at 480 pixels for a page that takes pictures only.

## Version 1 videos

The three Version 1 tutorials are on YouTube and are still the best moving pictures of the embossing action, which has not changed: loading the card with gravity's help, rolling about half way, turning the device over to watch the dots, and rolling back and forward over the rows, the card still held in the cylinders, until every dot is formed.

- Using the completed embosser: <https://youtu.be/77iXvJbkXh4>
- Generating cylinders in the web app: <https://youtu.be/m71VNZq28MI>
- Assembling Version 1 (the parts differ from Version 2): <https://youtu.be/x-uPNtT2Gqk>
